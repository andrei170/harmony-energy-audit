Why We Are Leading With The Grant And Not The Website
You said residential is dead and you want mid-size commercial. These are different problems.

Fixing a website captures demand that already exists. In a dead residential market there is less of it to capture, which is why a better site alone would not answer what you asked for.

The grant creates demand instead. A business owner who was not thinking about solar becomes interested when the arithmetic changes, and most of them have never heard of the programme. That is a reason to call you this year rather than consider solar in three years.

And it is defensible. Three better-built competitors are all selling installation quality and price. You are the only one of the four who has taken a commercial customer through the programme. That is a position, not a feature.

Said plainly, because it matters more than the pitch

We have not verified the current programme rules, eligibility or application windows, and we would check all of it against the published terms before a word went live. Grant terms change, and nothing in these documents is advice on eligibility or funding.

You have had a year of survival. That is the year to make spending accountable rather than bigger. The measurement work costs very little and it is what makes everything after it a decision instead of a guess.

The Order We Would Do It In
  • Put a phone link and an enquiry form on every page. An afternoon. Your site currently has no door at all and this should happen whatever else you decide.
  • Install Tag Manager and analytics. Before any spend. All three competitors already have it.
  • Build the REAP grant page. Who qualifies, what it funds, the application windows, what you handle. The one item on this list that generates commercial demand rather than competing for it.
  • Write meta descriptions and shorten the title so your company name survives in search results.
  • Fill out “Our Work” with real commercial installs — size, output, timescale, whether a grant was involved. The material already exists in your gallery, live data and news pages.
  • Then a commercial landing page written to a business owner, with the grant argument at the top and the panels further down.

Three better-built competitors. Not one of them is making your argument.

Move the grant to the front, give the page a door, and count what happens.
