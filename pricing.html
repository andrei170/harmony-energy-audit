The orphan angle may be worth more than the grant angle

You raised it and you were right. Sunbug bought out, Sunray gone owing you $150,000, Coastal Solar, and a dozen more. Their customers have nobody, and the larger firms have moved to utility scale and stopped caring about residential service.

And the roof point is the sharpest version of it: national outfits put arrays on roofs that should never have carried them, and five years on those people need a roof and cannot find anyone who will take the panels off and put them back. In your words, there is a big market for that.

Nobody is advertising against it. That is a whole category of work with no competition for the search, and it is service revenue on 600-700 customers you already have.

The arithmetic is not the hard part here

One job. Not one job a month, not one job a quarter. One job, once, at the smallest size you take, pays for the website and a full year of us running the advertising.

That is unusual and it is entirely because of your ticket size. For most businesses we look at, a year of this has to produce a steady stream of work before it washes its face. For you it has to produce a single additional commercial project in twelve months.

And you are running at six jobs a month against a capacity of twenty. The fourteen you are not doing are the ones this is aimed at.

We are deliberately not forecasting how many jobs this produces. You have been sold leads before by people who did forecast, and ninety percent of them said they had never heard of you. We are not doing that version.

Read this before trusting any number above

These are your figures, not measured by us. The 39% target, the 25% net, the job range and the capacity all came from you on the call. Nothing in your business currently records where a lead came from, which is exactly what Phase 1 fixes.

No number on this page is a prediction. It is arithmetic on what you told us, shown so you can see what would have to be true.
