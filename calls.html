Two Calls On One Page: What You Told Me, What I Showed You, And What Is Still Open.

Prepared for George Horrocks · AG1 Consulting · Written so that somebody who was on neither call can catch up in five minutes

The short version: you are doing six jobs a month, you could do twenty, and nobody can contact you from your own website.

Call one was me listening. Call two was me showing you a plan in two phases: fix the website first, then send people to it.

Nothing was signed and nothing was promised. You asked for time to think and to talk it over with your son.

Call one: your numbers • Call two: the plan • Then: what is left to settle
Call One, 29 September: What You Told Me
THE BUSINESS TODAY Jobs 6 a month or fewer What you could handle about 20 a month Year so far ~$600,000 Expected by year end ~$800,000 You used to run $3.5M - $4M a year Team 12, now on contracts Existing customers 600-700 Held in USDA grants $2,000,000 WHERE THE WORK COMES FROM Word of mouth and referral. Nothing else. Bought leads twice. "They were all garbage." WHAT YOU WANT This year's goal $1,000,000 Which is 1 to 5 more commercial jobs, at $80,000 - $250,000 each.
The four places the work could come from

1. Orphan systems. Seventeen installers you know of have gone, and their customers have nobody. You already service them.

2. Roofs. Panels that have to come off for a new roof and go back on. In your words, a big market.

3. Your own 600-700 customers, mostly inverter service.

4. The funding argument, REAP and CDFA, where you have more experience than the firms you compete with.

The first three need no grant to be released. We found no competitor advertising against any of them.

The full version, in your own words, is on Where You Are / Where You Want To Be.

Call Two, 30 September: What I Showed You
The plan is two phases, in this order

Phase 1 is the website. An enquiry form, a number people can tap, and tracking, so that somebody who finds you can reach you and you can see where they came from. You saw two working mock-ups.

Phase 2 is the traffic, and it starts only once Phase 1 is live. There is no point pouring water into a leaking bucket.

THE FIVE CAMPAIGN IDEAS, IN THE ORDER WE WOULD TEST THEM 1. Orphan systems "my solar installer went out of business" 2. Roof removal "remove solar panels for roof replacement" 3. Inverter service "solar inverter not working" 4. REAP and funding the offer is an eligibility check, not a grant 5. Commercial solar the most competition, and the biggest jobs 1, 2 and 3: nobody local is bidding. Firms elsewhere are, which is the evidence that people search for them. Before any money is spent on 3: one email to your own customers, to see how much appetite there is.
The two questions you asked, and the answers

"Most answers people get now come from AI, not a normal search. What does that do to this?"
The pages are written to be found both ways: by Google, and by the AI tools that answer questions directly. Same pages, written so either can read them.

"Is the new website editable? Is it WordPress?"
It can be. We can build it on WordPress if that is what you and your son prefer, and either way you get access to edit it yourselves.

The detail is on Campaign Ideas and Deliverables & Pricing, and the mock-ups are under Concept Sites.

Where You Landed
I ASKED WHICH WEBSITE OPTION YOU WERE LEANING TOWARDS. Complete. $2,500, one-off, half to start I ASKED WHICH WAY OF RUNNING THE TRAFFIC. "The cheapest." Steady: $1,000 a month, our management only, month to month, starting after the site is live AND THEN You wanted both website options in writing, time to compare them, and a conversation with your son before deciding.
What is still open

1. Your son's view. He built your first website and a newer one that was never put live. What should happen to that one?

2. Essential or Complete, and whether it is built on WordPress.

3. The list of the seventeen installers that have gone. The orphan pages and the first campaign are built from it.

4. The office side. I said I would show you a simple way to keep enquiries, quotes and jobs in one place, so nothing depends on your mobile phone. We have not looked at that yet, and it is not priced.
