The short version: lead with the service work that is already coming in (orphan systems, roofs, inverters), add small commercial as the second lane, build the site as proper multiple pages for Google with a one-page ad page for each campaign, and we run the tracking so it takes none of George’s time.

1. Who Is The Ideal Client?
Jaron’s point

“Your new site should be focusing on a target client, not necessarily who you have been working for in the past.” And: where is the money now, and where will it be in one, two or five years?

⭐ Lane 1, now: the service work nobody else wants

George put it at about 25% of last year’s business: new roofs needing panels off, inverters not working, systems with no communication. Almost all of it from people whose installer has gone. Another came in just before Friday’s call: a tracker system whose installer went out of business.

This is the fastest money because the demand is already there and nobody is advertising for it. It is why it comes first.

⭐ Lane 2, next: small commercial

George, on the call: even without the USDA money, a small commercial project still gets roughly 30% tax credit, about 24% from depreciation and around 10% for American-made products, about 64 to 65% in all, which he said puts the customer cash-positive in around three years.

Jaron raised commercial as the focus two to three years ago. This brings it back, at the size Harmony can build. It now has its own page on the new site.

Left out on purpose

Very large commercial, which needs a different kind of engineering, as George said. REAP stays as a page but not a campaign until the federal picture settles, with George’s $2 million of approved USDA grants having been held until less than a month ago.

2. Are These The Right Measures?
Jaron asked whether the audit items actually lead to customers

Fair challenge. The audit items (the form, tap-to-call, headings, keywords) are not the goal. They are what lets the goal be measured. The numbers that matter are three: inquiries per campaign, jobs booked from them, and the value of those jobs by type. Everything on the site is there to make those three visible.

3. Tracking Costs George No Time
George: “That’s not going to happen for me”

It does not need to. We set the tracking up and we read it. Every inquiry is labelled automatically with the page and campaign it came from, and George gets a short monthly summary. Nothing to log, nothing to learn.

4. One Page Or Many? Both, On One Domain
What I said on the call, now built

The main site is multi-page, so each service has its own page for Google and AI search to find. Each ad gets its own one-page landing page, with no menu, which loads fast and has one job. Both sit on harmonyenergyworks.com, for example /orphan-systems-help.
