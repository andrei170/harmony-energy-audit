Who I Am
AG1 Consulting

I am Andrei. We build websites and run advertising for trade businesses, so they can find work instead of waiting for it to come to them. George and I first spoke on 29 September.

Why We Are Talking, In George’s Words
The business from $3.5M to $4M a year down to about $600,000 The work about 6 jobs a month, all word of mouth, room for around 20 The crew twelve people, 20 years with George, now on as contractors The money USDA grant money held since late 2024 Bought leads tried, and in George’s words, “They were all garbage.” The goal around 20 jobs a month, about $1,000,000 a year, the crew back on steady work
What We Found On The Website
Graded F, 29 out of 100

No inquiry form, no number you can tap on a phone, and no tracking, so someone who finds Harmony has no easy way to get in touch and nobody can see where work comes from. The REAP and funding work, which nobody nearby is talking about, sits in the news section. Rechecked this week: the Contact Us link now goes to a page that does not exist.

The full scorecard is on the audit and the recheck is on Audit Pt.2.

The Plan George Put Together On 12 October
⭐ 1. Orphan systems

17 local installers have gone and left their customers with nobody to call. George gets those calls already: “A terrible need out there.” The idea is to go after that work rather than wait for it.

⭐ 2. Removal and reinstall

When a house is re-roofed, the panels have to come off and go back on. “There’s a big market for that.” One local firm has started advertising this since September (on Competitors Pt.2).

⭐ 3. An email to George’s own customers

Inverter service for the 600 to 700 existing customers. No advertising cost.

Later, and the area

REAP and funding on hold until Washington is clearer. Commercial solar last. The area: all of New Hampshire, all of Massachusetts, and southern Maine south of Portland.

The Website: Two Sizes
See both before the call

The smaller one: an inquiry form and tap-to-call on every page, tracking, the funding page out of the news section, and a new homepage.

The larger one: all of that, plus pages for orphan systems, removal and reinstall, inverter service, and commercial work.

These are mock-ups to show the idea, not finished sites. Whichever one is chosen can be built on WordPress so you and George can edit it. You built the current site, so your view on the platform is the one I most want today.

The cost

Everything is on Deliverables & Pricing. As we left it on 12 October: half of the website to start, the other half once the campaigns are bringing in work, the first month of management included, then month to month. The website belongs to Harmony either way.

What Is Left To Decide Today
1. Which size of website, and whether it goes on WordPress. 2. Yes or no on starting with the orphan-system and removal and reinstall pages. 3. The list of installers that have gone. The first campaign is built from it. 4. Any questions you have. Nothing is decided until you and George are both happy.
