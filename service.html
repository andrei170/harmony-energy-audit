Pool 1 — The Customers You Already Have
WHAT YOU TOLD US Existing customers 600-700 Inverter platform in the field SolarEdge Your description of them unreliable WHAT YOUR WEBSITE SAYS ABOUT SERVICE A service page none Anything about inverter replacement nothing Anything addressed to an existing customer at all nothing
An array lasts decades. The inverter does not.

You have six to seven hundred systems in the field on a platform you describe as unreliable. Every one of those inverters is a replacement at some point, and when it fails the customer is generating nothing while they wait.

You are already getting that work. It is arriving by phone and nothing records it. So it cannot be forecast, cannot be scheduled into quiet weeks, and cannot be shown to anyone as a revenue line.

What we would actually do with this, and it is not advertising
  • A service page, so the enquiry has somewhere to land that is not your mobile.
  • A single email to your own list. You own the relationship already — this costs postage of nothing and reaches people who have already paid you once.
  • Record it, so service becomes a number rather than an interruption. At the moment nobody could tell you what percentage of your revenue it is.

None of that needs a campaign, a budget or a grant.

This is the only finding in your whole document set with no competitor in it

Every other page we have sent you compares you to somebody better built. On this one there is nobody to compare to, because nobody is chasing it.

The larger firms have moved toward utility-scale work and away from looking after individual systems. In your words, as they move to one, two and five megawatt projects, "they don't care about their customers anymore." That leaves several hundred households with a system, a fault, and no one to ring.

You named ReVision specifically as having largely left residential, and said the other big name up north is in the same position. Those are not just competitors vacating a market. They are the source of the customers in this pool.

You are already the firm they end up finding. The difference between that and a business line is a page that says so.

What we are not going to pretend

We do not know how many customers those seventeen firms left behind, and we are not going to estimate it. Seventeen companies is your figure, not a measurement of ours.

Sunray owing you $150,000 is a debt, not a market. Nothing on this page recovers it and we are not suggesting it does.

This is a search nobody is answering

Somebody in that position has a specific, urgent and expensive problem, and they are not comparing three solar quotes. They are trying to find anyone at all who will touch it.

It is also a job with two trades in it, which is precisely why nobody wants it and precisely why whoever will do it has no competition for the enquiry.

The question we could not answer for you

Do you do the roof yourselves, or only the removal and refit of the array? That changes what the page says and who it is written for.

If you coordinate with a roofer, saying so plainly is a stronger offer than either trade alone — because the customer's actual problem is that two trades keep pointing at each other.
