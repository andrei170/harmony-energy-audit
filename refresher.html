His pain, in his words

"All of my guys right now, who have worked for me for 20 years, are just as contractors now, because we just have no big things coming in." Twelve people. He said twice it is not good for them. This is the real reason, not money for himself: "I'm 73 years old. So I don't care either way."

2. Work only comes by word of mouth

About six jobs a month ("we're lucky if we get half a dozen") against capacity of about 20. No form, no tap-to-call, no tracking on the site, so nobody who finds him can easily reach him.

3. Money is tight

About $148k in debt, solar down about 25% this year, $2M of approved USDA grants stalled. Said he did not want to go deeper in the hole.

4. He has been burned before

Bought leads from Solar Leads, Angie's List and others. "They were all garbage." About 90% said they never asked to be contacted.

Lost a few commercial jobs to ReVision, who used to stay out of commercial. New since we last spoke (checked 18 Nov): New England Clean Energy now has a "Solar Panel Removal and Reinstallation" page for Rockingham County NH and York County ME, and says it services systems other firms installed.
