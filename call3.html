What You Told Me That Matters For The Pages
⭐ The deadline that makes commercial urgent

The 30% tax credit and the depreciation end with 2027, and the system has to be completed and live by then. A business owner thinking about solar “in a couple of years” has already run out of time. That goes at the top of the commercial page.

⭐ The RECs, in your numbers

About $27 net per 1,000 kWh a year. A 100 kW system producing about 125,000 kWh brings roughly $3,500 a year on top of the free electricity, and with USDA funding and RECs a commercial system can be cash positive in one to three years. Almost nobody explains this to a business owner, so the commercial page will.

The orphan systems, in your words

A customer in Greenville, Massachusetts had three optimizers out on a large parking garage and no idea which ones, because the installer never did the map. “Installers get lazy.” Solar Wolf is one more name for the list of firms that have gone.
