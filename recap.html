What You Told Me, What I Got Wrong, And What Changed Because Of It.

Prepared for George Horrocks · AG1 Consulting · Before we speak again

You corrected me twice on that call and both corrections changed the plan.

I came in with a brief that said the residential market was dead and you were sitting on money you could not use. You told me that was wrong, and what you said instead is now the centre of everything I have sent you.

This page exists so you can see I was listening, and so nothing has to be repeated.

Your figures, in your words • One correction of mine at the bottom • Nothing here is measured by AG1
The Two Things I Had Wrong
WHAT I WAS BRIEFED "Residential space is pretty dead. Holding millions they aren't able to use." WHAT YOU ACTUALLY SAID "No. We have $2 million in USDA grants that are being withheld by the federal government." Held since late 2024. Three jobs in your queue are sitting on it right now.
Those are not the same problem at all

A dead market means you need new demand. Withheld funding means the demand exists and cannot complete. Everything I would have proposed on the first reading would have been aimed at the wrong thing.

The second correction was smaller and just as useful. I had you compared against Sunergy Solutions, and you told me they are out of business. I have a correction of my own to make on that one and it is at the bottom of this page.

Why I have not sent you a lead generation pitch

You have bought leads twice and both times the people on the other end had not asked to hear from anybody. Proposing a third version of that would be ignoring the clearest thing you told me.

The gap is not demand, it is that you are running at under a third of what you can build. Fourteen jobs a month of unused capacity is the number everything else should be measured against.

I went away and checked whether anybody is competing for it

Granite State Solar and ReVision Energy say nothing about either on the pages we measured. We could not find a firm advertising panel removal and refit at all.

That is the only part of this whole piece of work with no competitor in it, and it is the only part that needs neither advertising spend nor the federal money released. It has its own page now: The Revenue You Already Own.

And the third pool, which is the quietest

Six to seven hundred existing customers on SolarEdge inverters you described as unreliable. An array lasts decades and an inverter does not. That work is already arriving by phone and nothing records it, so it cannot be forecast or scheduled into a quiet week.

The argument you already own is the one nobody can find

You have prior REAP and CDFA experience and your competitors do not mention funding at all. It is the one thing on the page where you lead, and it is filed under news.

Nobody who lands on that site can contact you without picking up a phone, and three of the four revenue pools above have no page pointing at them.

A Correction Of My Own
YOU TOLD ME SUNERGY SOLUTIONS ARE OUT OF BUSINESS I checked their domain mid-morning on 30 September: HTTP 522 -- server did not answer I wrote that "the server agrees" with you. I checked again before sending: HTTP 200. Site fully live. 3,713 words. Certificate valid into December 2026.
So I was wrong, and I would rather say so before you repeat it

A 522 means a server did not answer for a moment. It does not mean a company has closed, and I should not have written it that way.

Your account of their position stands as yours. I have not checked any companies register and I cannot corroborate it from a website that is working. Both can be true at once, because a live site outlives a company by months.

The competitor page has been rewritten accordingly, and none of the orphan argument depended on it.

Where That Leaves Us
  • The grant work is real and you cannot move it. Two million held since late 2024, three jobs waiting on it. Worth having, not worth planning around.
  • The other three pools need no money and no permission — your own 600 to 700 customers, the orphans, and the roof refits.
  • Nothing can be measured until the site can take an enquiry, which is the whole of Phase 1.
  • Count last month's service work before deciding anything. Not estimate it, count it. That number does not currently exist and everything on the service page is an argument until it does.
  • And decide the roof question — whether you take the roof on yourselves or coordinate it. It changes who that page is written for.

Fourteen jobs a month of unused capacity, and six hundred customers nobody has written to.

None of that waits on Washington.
