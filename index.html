Finding 1 — The REAP Grant Is The Commercial Lead Generator You Already Own
WHERE "REAP" APPEARS ON YOUR SITE /our-work/in-the-news 6 mentions -- a news page, 1,146 words / 1 mention -- buried in the homepage /solar-101 0 /our-work 0 /our-team 0 There is no commercial page. There is no grant page.
This is the whole strategy, and it is already yours

A mid-size business owner is not searching for solar panels. They are looking for a capital project that pays for itself. A USDA REAP grant changes that arithmetic completely, and most business owners in rural New Hampshire and Maine have never heard of it.

You have secured them before. That is a specific, checkable, hard-to-copy credential — and it sits on a page called “In The News”, which is where websites put things they have finished caring about.

A page built around the grant is a commercial lead generator, not a brochure page. Who qualifies, what it covers, when the application windows open, what you handle. That page answers a question a business owner is actually asking, and it is the one thing on this list that creates demand rather than capturing it.

Finding 2 — There Is No Way To Contact You From The Page
ON YOUR HOMEPAGE YOU Sunergy ReVision Granite State Enquiry form none yes yes yes Click-to-call link none yes yes yes
This is the cheapest fix on the list and the one costing you most

Every competitor we measured lets a visitor call or write from the homepage. Yours does neither. A business owner who reads your page and decides to act has to go and find a number somewhere else, and a proportion of them simply will not.

It does not matter how good the rest of the site is if the page has no door. This is an afternoon's work and it should happen whatever else you decide.

Finding 3 — Nothing Is Measuring Anything
ANALYTICS / TAGS FOUND YOU Sunergy ReVision Granite State Google Tag Manager none yes yes yes Google Analytics 4 none none none none Meta pixel none yes none none Google Ads conversion none none none none
After the year you have had, this matters more than usual

You told us this year has been about survival. That is exactly the year in which you cannot afford to spend money on marketing you are unable to measure — and exactly the year most firms get sold something unmeasurable.

All three competitors run Tag Manager. You run nothing. So they can at least see what their pages do. Before any budget goes anywhere, this is the piece that makes the spend accountable.

Description ABSENT -- Google selects a sentence from the page itself. Canonical absent Schema absent Homepage 466 words · 1 H1 · 1 H2 /our-work 100 words -- your proof page is nearly empty
The credential is right. The packaging is not.

Leading the title with NABCEP is a genuinely good instinct — it is the credential that separates a real installer from a sales outfit. But at eighty characters, the part that gets cut is your name.

And “Our Work” at a hundred words is the page a commercial buyer visits before calling you. You have a gallery, live data and news, so the material exists. It is just not on the page where somebody deciding whether to trust you will look.

What Is Already Working
NABCEP certification stated clearly and put in the title USDA REAP grant experience rare, checkable, hard to copy Real site depth Solar 101, team, gallery, live data, news Live production data almost nobody publishes this Three-state coverage NH, Maine and Massachusetts
Why this is a packaging problem, not a business problem

You have the credential, the track record and the grant experience. Most firms we audit have to build that from nothing. Yours exists and is simply not arranged where a commercial buyer will find it.

Nothing here requires a rebuild. It requires a contact route, a measurement tag, a description and one new page about the grant.

The Order We Would Do It In
  • Put a phone link and an enquiry form on every page. An afternoon, and it is the door your site currently does not have.
  • Install Tag Manager and analytics. Before any spend. All three competitors already have it.
  • Build the REAP grant page. Who qualifies, what it funds, application windows, what you handle. This is the commercial lead generator and nobody else in your field is publishing it.
  • Write meta descriptions and shorten the title so your name survives.
  • Fill out “Our Work” with real commercial installations — size, saving, timescale. The material already exists across your gallery and news pages.
  • Then a commercial landing page written to a business owner rather than a homeowner, with the grant argument at the top.

You already hold the argument that wins commercial solar. It is filed under news.

Move it to the front, give the page a door, and put something in place to count what happens.
