Six columns where you are the only “no”

Description, canonical, schema, Tag Manager, form, phone link. On every one of those, all three of the firms measured have it and you do not. None of them is a difficult or expensive item.

The word count gap is real but it is the least urgent thing here. A page with 2,790 words and no way to contact you would still be worse than a 500-word page with a phone number on it.

Finding 1 — ReVision Proves You Do Not Need More Words
ReVision Energy 774 words · 6 H2 · description · canonical schema · Tag Manager · form · click-to-call You 466 words · 1 H2 · none of the above
The nearest competitor by size is 774 words, not 2,790

ReVision is one of the best known solar names in northern New England and their homepage is 774 words. That is three hundred more than yours. It is not a content mountain.

What separates them is entirely the plumbing — a description, a canonical, schema, a tag, a form and a phone link. Every one of those is a settings-and-afternoon job rather than a writing project.

So the realistic first target is ReVision, and now that Sunergy has gone dark it is the only target that matters. Match the plumbing and add three hundred words and you are structurally level with the biggest name on this list.

Finding 2 — The Best-Built Site We Measured Belongs To A Company That Is Gone

The server agrees.
This changes the shape of the whole page

We built this comparison on 28 September and Sunergy was the strongest site in it — nearly three thousand words aimed at a single search phrase, a Meta pixel, the lot. Then you told us they had gone.

We re-checked it this morning and the domain now returns a 522, which means the front door answers but nothing is behind it. That is what a site looks like when the hosting is still paid and the company is not there.

So they are not a competitor. They are a vacancy. Whatever attention that page was collecting for “solar installers New Hampshire” is going to move, and the firms positioned for it will take it.

What this does and does not tell us

A 522 means the server did not respond when we asked. It is strong corroboration, not a certificate. A site can 522 for a day and come back. We have not checked any companies register.

And a dead site does not vacate its rankings immediately. Search engines keep showing pages for weeks or months after the business behind them stops. This is an opening, not an empty field.

The wider point is the one you made, not us: seventeen firms you service have gone the same way. This is one of them, and it happens to be the one we had you measured against.

Finding 3 — Nobody Is Talking About The Grant
USDA REAP / COMMERCIAL GRANT POSITIONING Sunergy Solutions not present on the page measured Granite State Solar not present on the page measured ReVision Energy not present on the page measured Harmony Energy Works REAP mentioned 6 times -- on a news page
This is the only column where you lead, and it is the one that matters for commercial

Three competitors, all better built than you, and not one of them is making the grant argument on the pages we measured. They are competing on installation quality and price, which is a crowded fight.

You have the credential and the history. A business owner choosing between four solar quotes is choosing on price. A business owner shown that a grant could fund a significant share of the project is having a completely different conversation — and only one firm can have it with them.

That is the position worth taking, and the reason it is available is that everyone else is selling panels.

Finding 4 — Nobody Can Prove What Their Advertising Does
GOOGLE ADS CONVERSION TAG (AW-) Harmony Energy Works none Sunergy Solutions none Granite State Solar none ReVision Energy none Four firms. Zero conversion tracking.
The whole market is spending on faith

Three of the four run Tag Manager, so they can see traffic. None can tie a commercial enquiry back to the page or the ad that produced it.

In a year where you have had to watch every dollar, that is the argument for doing this properly rather than doing it bigger. Measurement first is cheaper than spend, and it is available to you before any of them think to close the gap.

Finding 5 — The Orphan Systems Have No Competition At All, And It Was Your Idea

This is the only finding on the page with no competitor in it

Every other finding is a gap between you and somebody better built. This one has nobody in it. Not Granite State, not ReVision, and the third name on our list has joined the orphans rather than chasing them.

The demand exists and it is already reaching you — you are servicing these systems now. What is missing is a page saying so, which is why none of it compounds.

And the roof version is sharper still. National outfits put arrays on roofs that should never have carried them; five years on those owners need a roof and cannot find anybody who will lift the panels and put them back. You will. Nobody is advertising that.

Why this is worth more than the grant page

The grant argument depends on Washington releasing money that has been held since late 2024. The orphan argument depends on nothing. Those systems are already broken and those owners are already looking.

It is also service revenue against 600-700 customers you already hold, with unreliable inverters among them. That is the cheapest demand on this page, and it needs a page rather than a budget.

What This Field Tells Us To Do
  • Fix the plumbing first — form, phone link, description, canonical, schema, tag. Six items, all cheap, and they close six of the columns you are last in.
  • Build the orphan-system and roof-refit pages before anything else that costs money. It is the one finding here with no competitor in it, and the demand is already arriving at your door unrecorded.
  • Target ReVision's 774 words. Three hundred more words puts you structurally level with the best known name here.
  • Build the REAP grant page and own the only position nobody else is taking. It is the one thing on this list that generates commercial demand rather than competing for it.
  • Fill out “Our Work” — 100 words is not a proof page, and commercial buyers check it before calling.
  • Install measurement before any spend. All four of you are blind on conversions today.

Three better-built sites, and not one of them is making your argument.

The grant is the position. Everything else on this list is plumbing.
